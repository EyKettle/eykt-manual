# Contribution Guidebook

Emmm, weird, why would somebody want to contribute to this project?
Well, perhaps that's what piqued your curiosity, leading you to open this contribution guide?

Anyway, wellcome to contribute!

This guide serves to anchor the operational workflow, providing a standardized approach to project maintenance.

> [!NOTE]
> This guide is not yet complete and will continue to be updated over time.

## Actions Before Commits

Before committing your changes, ensure that the output of each unit of work is clean.
Run `pnpm lint` and resolve any errors or warnings that arise.
